Frequently asked questions

Everything you're probably wondering

How long does setup and onboarding take?+
Most hospitals are live within a week. Signup, hospital profile, plan selection, and staff invitations are guided steps inside the app — there's no separate implementation project to schedule.
Is our data isolated from other hospitals on Aiventra?+
Yes. Aiventra is built multi-tenant from the schema up — every record carries its owning hospital's identity, checked on every request, on every one of the 28 hospital-owned data tables. See our Security page for the full breakdown.
Can Aiventra staff see our patient data?+
Not by default. Aiventra support can request access with a stated reason, but only your own hospital admin can approve it, and every approval expires automatically.
What happens if we need more beds or users than our plan allows?+
You can upgrade to a higher plan, or add individual users, doctors, or branches to your current plan for a per-unit fee. Changes take effect as soon as payment is confirmed.
How is billing handled — monthly or annual?+
Both. Annual billing includes roughly two months free compared to paying monthly. You can switch billing cycles at your next renewal.
What happens to our data if we cancel?+
Your data remains fully accessible through the end of your current billing period. Export is available on request at any time, including after cancellation.
Do you support multiple branches under one hospital group?+
Yes, branch limits depend on your plan, with the Enterprise plan supporting unlimited branches. Each branch's data is isolated the same way every hospital's data is isolated from every other.
Is there a mobile app?+
Aiventra runs as a responsive web application that works on phones, tablets and desktops through the browser — no separate app installation needed today.
