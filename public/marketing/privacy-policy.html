This page is a working draft outlining the shape of our privacy commitments. It is not a substitute for review by qualified legal counsel, and specific regulatory details (such as our GSTIN, data-protection officer contact, and jurisdiction-specific clauses) will be finalized before public launch rather than published here as placeholders.

What we collect

To provide the Aiventra Hospital Management System, we collect: (a) account and hospital profile information provided at signup; (b) patient and clinical data your hospital's staff enter into the system, which belongs to and is controlled by your hospital, not Aiventra; (c) billing and subscription information needed to process payments via Razorpay; and (d) basic usage data needed to operate and secure the platform.

How hospital data is used

Patient and clinical data entered by your staff is used solely to provide the service to your hospital. It is not shared with, or made visible to, any other hospital on the platform. Aiventra staff do not have standing access to this data — see our Security page for how approval-gated access works.

Payment information

Subscription payments are processed by Razorpay. Aiventra does not store your card or bank account details — only the payment status and transaction reference returned by Razorpay after a verified payment.

Data retention

Your hospital's data remains accessible for the duration of your subscription and through the end of a cancelled subscription's paid period. Export is available on request.
