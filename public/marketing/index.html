Move patients through the front desk faster

  • Search existing patients by name, mobile, or ID in one box
  • Register OPD visits and assign doctors in a few taps
  • Print token and receipt immediately at the counter

Spend consultation time on patients, not paperwork

  • See your own queue, ordered by token, with wait times
  • Record diagnosis, vitals and prescriptions against the visit
  • Recommend admission directly into the bed allocation flow

Run the hospital, not just watch it

  • Bed occupancy, daily revenue and OPD volume on one dashboard
  • Manage staff roles and permissions without touching code
  • Full audit log of every clinical and billing action taken

  • Lab orders move through collection, processing, and finalized report
  • Pharmacy staff see prescriptions the moment doctors write them
  • Results reach the doctor's queue automatically once finalized

No more phone calls to ask "is the report ready yet?" — the status is always visible.
