Built multi-tenant from day one

Every hospital's data, provably isolated from every other

Aiventra was designed as a multi-tenant platform from the first line of the schema, not retrofitted onto a single-hospital system later. Here's exactly how that isolation works.

🔒

Every record carries its owning hospital's identity

Patients, admissions, bills, lab orders, files — every one of the 28 hospital-owned tables carries an organization identifier, checked on every single read, write, update, and delete.

🔐

The hospital is never taken from what the browser sends

Which hospital a request belongs to is derived only from the signed-in session on the server — never from a URL parameter, form field, or anything else a browser could be made to send.

👤

Ten distinct staff roles, enforced on the server

From receptionist to organization owner, every role's permissions are checked on the backend for every action — hiding a button in the interface is never the only protection.

📁

Files are isolated the same way data is

Uploaded documents, receipts and reports live in a per-hospital storage path, and every download independently verifies the requester belongs to that hospital before the file is ever opened.

📜

A full audit trail, visible to your own admins

Every clinical and billing action is logged with who performed it and when. Your hospital's own administrators can review it at any time — no request to Aiventra needed.

⏰

Aiventra staff need your approval to see your data

Aiventra support does not have standing access to any hospital's clinical data. A support engineer can request access with a stated reason, but only your own hospital admin can approve it — and every approval expires automatically, typically within a few hours.
