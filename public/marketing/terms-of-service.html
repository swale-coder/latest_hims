The service

Aiventra provides hospital management software on a subscription basis, described at Pricing. Your organization is responsible for the accuracy of data your staff enter into the system.

Subscriptions and billing

Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each billing period unless cancelled. Payments are processed via Razorpay; a subscription is only activated after payment is verified. Cancelling stops future renewals — access continues through the end of the period already paid for.

Data ownership

All patient and clinical data entered by your hospital's staff remains your hospital's property. Aiventra acts as a processor of that data on your behalf, not an owner of it.

Acceptable use

Accounts are for use by the hospital, clinic, or nursing home that registered them. Staff accounts should not be shared between individuals, and access should be limited to what each role genuinely requires.

Changes to these terms

We will notify registered hospital administrators of material changes to these terms before they take effect.
